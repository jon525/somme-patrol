Flying notes

Your shadow shows how high you are. A bomb carries forward with you and lands under your shadow, so release early from height. Guns only hit aircraft at your own altitude; the altitude readout turns red when one is level with you. Fly below 14 to strafe tanks, guns, boats and houses. Bridges and headquarters need bombs.

Damage letters

  • FFuel leak. The tank drains twice as fast.
  • BBomb gear. Half your bombs hang up.
  • MManoeuvring. Controls go sluggish.
  • GGun. Slow rate of fire.

A fifth hit brings you down. Landing repairs everything.
