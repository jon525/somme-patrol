# Somme Patrol

A diagonal-scrolling First World War biplane shooter for the browser, in the spirit of *Blue Max* (Synapse Software, 1983).

You fly a Sopwith Camel up a river valley. Bomb eight flashing targets, then find the city and destroy its three headquarters. Land on a friendly airstrip to refuel, rearm and repair.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. It is a single file with no build step and no dependencies; it loads two fonts from Google Fonts.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Up / W | Climb. Hold it on the strip to take off. |
| Down / S | Dive. Touch down on a strip to land. |
| Left / Right (A / D) | Bank across the valley. |
| Space / Z | Machine gun |
| X / B | Drop a bomb |
| P | Pause |
| M | Mute |

On touch screens, on-screen buttons appear below the playfield.

## Flying notes

- Your shadow shows your altitude. A dotted line drops from each plane to the ground.
- Guns only hit aircraft at your own altitude. The altitude readout turns red when an enemy is level with you.
- Fly below 14 to strafe tanks, anti-aircraft guns, boats and houses. Bridges and headquarters need bombs.
- A bomb carries forward with you and lands under your shadow, so release early from height.

## Damage

| Letter | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| F | Fuel leak. The tank drains twice as fast. |
| B | Bomb gear. Half your bombs hang up. |
| M | Manoeuvring. Controls go sluggish. |
| G | Gun. Slow rate of fire. |

A fifth hit brings you down. Landing repairs everything. You have three planes.
